What ClearlyCompliant Does and Doesn't Cover

ClearlyCompliant is a UK GDPR compliance checker that scans your website's public-facing pages for common compliance indicators. It checks for things like cookie consent mechanisms, privacy policies, security headers, and tracking scripts — the issues most commonly identified in ICO investigations of websites.

It does not assess internal data handling processes, HR data practices, paper-based data collection, or offline activities. It is designed to identify the most common website-level compliance gaps quickly and affordably, and is not a substitute for professional legal advice.
