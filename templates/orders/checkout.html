Most Businesses Don't Know What They're Missing

GDPR compliance isn't just about having a privacy policy. There are dozens of requirements — and regulators are actively looking for violations.

⚠️

Hidden Tracking Scripts

Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel and other trackers on your site may require explicit consent you're not collecting.

📄

Inadequate Privacy Policies

Having a privacy policy isn't enough — it must cover everything your site actually does. Most don't.

🍪

Cookie Consent Issues

Pre-ticked boxes, no opt-out, and missing cookie policies are among the most commonly fined GDPR violations.

🔒

Security Header Gaps

Missing security headers leave user data vulnerable and signal to regulators that data protection isn't a priority.
